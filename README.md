# bat_grazing_lawns

This repository includes analytical code and date used in the following manuscript:

From Grass to Sky: Grazing Ecosystem Engineers Create Aerial Predator-Prey Hotspots

Understanding the myriad of ways in which ecosystem engineers shape ecosystems is essential for assessing their ecological role and the consequences of their population declines. Grazing megaherbivores such as white rhinoceros and hippopotamus, both in decline, frequently form grazing lawns in savanna systems. While studies on the impact of megaherbivore-formed grazing lawns have largely focused on terrestrial species, we found that grazing lawns are hotspots for nocturnal winged insects and their aerial predators (bats) and contain significantly higher nocturnal winged insect abundance and bat activity, compared to adjacent savanna habitats. Further declines in grazing megaherbivores will likely reduce the abundance and distribution of grazing lawns, which may have cascading effects for these aerial hotspots.

# Content  
_Code_  
R markdown_bat grazing lawn code.Rmd: Rmarkdown script that contains the code for the analyses used in the manuscript

_Data_  
- Bat species comp.csv: collected data on bat species composition that is used in the multivariate analyses.  
- Bat species comp metadata.csv: metadata associated with the bat species composition data. Again, this was used in the multivariate analyses.  
- Bat species richness and abundance.csv: collected data on bat species richness and abundnace. Bat activity (# of calls) was used instead of abundance.  

- Insect species comp.csv: collected data on insect species composition that is used in the multivariate analyses.  
- Insect species comp metadata.csv: metadata associated with the insect species composition data. Again, this was used in the multivariate analyses.  
- Insect grazing lawn abundance and richness.csv: collected data on insect species richness and abundnace.  

- Grazing lawn ave grass height data.csv: grass height measurements between treatments (grazing lawns and adjacent savanna controls).  
- Grazing lawn tree density data.csv: tree count data between treatments (grazing lawns and adjacent savanna controls).
